## The Category

*This is the broader theme and area you are playing in. What domain are you surfacing opportunities for? Each of you should have the same category for your team (we provided it to you!).*

## The Actors

*This is a breakdown of the stakeholders that are important to be aware. Who makes up the system within a particular thematic area? Per Actor - what are our early hypotheses about their motivations? How is value being exchanged here? Doing so here anchors all the work around human-centered needs, behaviors and motivations.*

## The Situation

*This is the section to set the context and create the ultimate frame around where you are exploring. It might involve describing the current state of the market, particular behaviors of a stakeholder or a part of the value chain and how it works. You can think about it as the "once upon a time" part of a story - it sets the scene. Some of the elements to consider when crafting a situation:*

- *Background - Enough information for us to understand the context. Historical information, current state, stats.*
- *Relevance - Make it clear why the situation matters. Explain the importance within the theme, to the stakeholders, etc. Why does it matter?*
- *Keep it clear and simple - this is really important. The situation should be easy to grok for other strategists, to get them in the right mindset.*

## The Complication

*This is the turning point, the climax, the disruption to the situation that was set up previously. This is the problem, challenge or change that ultimately is creating tension within the ecosystem of the "Situation", thus warranting exploration in the first place. Some ways to think about elements of the complication:*

- *Disruption - The complication needs to represent enough change from the initial situation to cause issues (could be internal or external).*
- *Relevance - The complication needs to flow from the situation and be clearly linked to it. These are macro or micro forces affecting the world you've built in the situation.*
- *Urgency - should set the tone that this is something that warrants addressing. Something is changing, something is happening that's causing conflict and it's important to have it addressed.*

## The Target Outcome

*If your venture is successful, this is what we are looking to move the needle on because we believe doing so will help address the complication that's been setup. In many ways, the target outcome somewhat sets up the purpose of whatever venture is ultimately developed. Target outcomes should be directional, measurable and relevant to the complication and contribute to resolving the initial situation that was setup.*

## Potential Solutions

*This is a collection of potential ideas that you've come up with that could exist within this opportunity. At this stage they are directional in nature, don't be too precious with them - they are purely there to help inspire others and ground them in what you are working on.*

## The Reason to Believe

*Why should the parent company you are serving believe that this foundational work is worth believing at all? This is the section where desktop and secondary research is presented to establish evidence that the situation is a thing and that the complication is happening and that moving the needle on the target outcome will address the established situation/complication.*
