## Its flywheel

*A flywheel is a reinforcing loop: each turn makes the next turn easier. More of A leads to more of B, which leads to more of C, which leads back to more of A. From the lecture: Jim Collins (Good to Great) on compounding good decisions; the NFX flywheel examples (Amazon, Stripe, Vanguard, Cleveland Clinic, Windows); reinforcing loops from the week 5 systems-thinking readings. Draw it, then write it.*
