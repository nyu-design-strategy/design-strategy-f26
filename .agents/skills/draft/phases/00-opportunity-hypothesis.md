## Voice

Office-hours notes quote the student verbatim under "What you said". Those quotes are the raw material. A Situation built from *"my aunt drove 40 minutes to the only funeral home that does green burials"* is better than one built from *"consumers increasingly seek sustainable options."* Keep the aunt.
