# Phase 01 · Competitors and Flywheel, plus the team downselect

**Due:** the exact date and time is in your team's Slack channel.

Three things are due, two of them as a team. From Lecture 5:

1. **Individually:** figure out 10 competitors in the market for your final project topic and choose the biggest one to draw a flywheel for it.
2. **Group:** down select two opportunity hypotheses, with a short description of each and why you selected it.
3. **Group:** define the types of people and how you will recruit them to inform your opportunity hypothesis research.

Items 2 and 3 go in one team file and one team submission.

## Your folder (individual)

```
01-competitors-flywheel/
└── <your-netid>-<your-name>/     same name as your phase 00 folder
    ├── draft.md        /start sets it up, /draft fills it from your notes
    ├── notes.md        every skill and conversation writes here
    ├── flywheel.png    your drawing (any tool, or a screenshot)
    └── sessions/       optional
```

Type `/start` (Claude Code) or `$start` (Codex) and it creates the folder. Your phase 00 folder stays as it is.

## The team file (group)

```
team/
├── 01-downselect.md    the two hypotheses, the people, the recruiting plan
└── notes.md            the team's session log
```

One person opens the repo in Claude Code or Codex during the team meeting and types `/team`. Everyone talks; that person types. It reads every teammate's Opportunity Hypothesis, asks the team the questions one at a time, and writes the file in the team's words, with who said what. It never picks for you.

## Suggested workflow

**Individual.** `/office-hours` first: it asks which market, exactly, who the customer chooses between today, which one is biggest and by what measure, and what gets stronger as that company grows. `/research` for the competitor facts and size numbers; every number needs a source page. `/draft` fills the template, including the loop in words. Draw the flywheel in any tool, or screenshot the diagram `/draft` generates once it's on GitHub, and save it as `flywheel.png` in your folder. `/pressure-test`, then `/submit`.

**Team.** Everyone submits their individual Opportunity Hypothesis first, or at least has a draft. Meet. One person runs `/team`. When the file is written, the same person (or anyone) runs `/submit` and says it's the team's submission. One link per team in Slack.

## Sections

**Individual:** Category · The market · Ten competitors · The biggest one · Its flywheel (drawing, loop in words, what slows it, where my hypothesis sits) · AI use. Template: `course/templates/competitors-flywheel.md`.

**Team:** The hypotheses we considered · The two we're carrying forward · Who we need to talk to · How we'll recruit them · AI use. Template: `course/templates/team-downselect.md`.
