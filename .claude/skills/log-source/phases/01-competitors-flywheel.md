# log-source · Phase 01: Competitors and Flywheel

Most sources in this phase are size signals for competitors: revenue, users, locations, funding. Notes for this phase:

- **Company pages are `marketing`.** A competitor's own "about" page, press release, or investor deck is logged as `marketing` and the Caveats say so. Filings (10-K, annual report) are `filing`.
- **Record the measure, not just the number.** "$2.1B" means nothing without "revenue, FY2025, global". Put the measure, the year, and the geography in **The number**.
- **Year of data goes in the filename.** A 2026 article quoting FY2024 revenue is `2024-<slug>.md`.
- **Fit check for this phase:** does the number measure what the student's "biggest by" claim needs? Revenue vs. bookings vs. gross merchandise value vs. users are different measures; flag a mismatch.
- **Used for** phase column: `01-competitors-flywheel`.

If the student pastes a list of competitors with no sources, that's not a log-source job. Say so, and point to `/research` to find the numbers.
