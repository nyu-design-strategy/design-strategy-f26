# log-source · Phase 00: Opportunity Hypothesis

## Phase context

The Opportunity Hypothesis is individual, but `sources/` is shared by the whole team. Sources logged now get reused in the team downselect (right after this phase) and in the Research Readout (week 9). So it's worth getting **What was measured** right the first time: the teammate who reuses this page in week 9 won't have read the original.

## Notes for this phase

- Students at this stage often reach for a big market-size number first. Log it if they want, but ask which specific person the number describes. A total-addressable-market figure rarely says anything about the one person from their office-hours notes.
- Sources most useful now are the ones that show **what changed** (a regulation, a price shift, a new technology, a demographic trend) and **what people do today** (workarounds, existing services). If the student only brings market-size reports, mention that once.
- The claim in **Used for** should be phrased the way it will appear in their reasoning, not as a topic. "Workers are paid every two weeks but rent is due monthly" is a claim. "Payment timing" is a topic.
- Sections of the deliverable that lean on evidence are the Situation, the Complication, and the Reason to Believe. Don't write for those sections; do make sure the claim recorded in **Used for** is specific enough to be checked later.
